A new look, after Finery.

- Soft grey pages, white rounded tiles and one blue accent, in light and dark.
- New type: Inter for English and IBM Plex Sans Arabic for Arabic, with bold, tight headings.
- A fetched video fills the top of the screen. The quality picker and Download sit at the bottom, in reach of your thumb.
- Quality, language and theme are picked with iOS-style segmented controls.
- Home shows the apps Nazzil works with, all alike.
- The app icon and the launch screen are white, with the logo's dots in the new blue.

## Installing

- **Android**: uninstall the old Nazzil first. This build is signed with a new key, so Android will not install it over 2.4. What you saved to the gallery stays; the list inside Nazzil starts empty.
- **iPhone**: the IPA is unsigned. Install it with Sideloadly, AltStore or SideStore, which sign it with your Apple ID.
- **Windows**: the installer is not signed, so SmartScreen may warn. Choose More info, then Run anyway.
- **Mac**: open the disk image and drag Nazzil onto Applications. The app is not notarised: the first time it is blocked, open System Settings, Privacy & Security, and choose Open Anyway.
- **Linux**: install the .deb with `sudo apt install ./Nazzil-2.5.0-linux-amd64.deb`, or make the AppImage executable with `chmod +x` and run it. Both need Ubuntu 24.04, Debian 13, Fedora 40 or newer.
