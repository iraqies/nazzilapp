# antislop fix 001

Date: 2026-11-10. Mode: AFTER. Follow-up to `audit-001-2026-11-10.md`. The owner approved all 31 findings.

Scope: the Flutter app in `flutter/`, commits ecf433c to b3c8a58 on the branch `cursor/nazzil-fix-a100`. `main` is untouched.

How it was checked: 121 automated tests pass (`flutter test`) and the analyzer reports no issues. The Linux build was run and clicked through at 412 x 915 in both themes and both languages, and at 1320 x 920. The Android APK was built from the same commit and inspected: package `com.nazzil.app`, version 1.3 (22), arm64, and every icon the code names is in its icon font. The APK was not run, because the build machine has no phone and no emulator.

## The 31 findings

1. Fixed. Text and icons on a colour fill are ink `#0B0B0C` in all five colours: the main button, the active tab, the picked choice. `theme_contrast_test.dart` measures every colour in both themes.
2. Fixed. The second colour is gone (finding 27), so nothing sits on it. Save photos, the picked Photos choice and the main button of the done dialog use the fill and ink of finding 1.
3. Fixed. The light theme has its own shades. Ready is muted text, coloured text is a darker shade of the picked hue, error text is `#B3202C`, muted text is `#625D58`. The corner glow is gone (finding 18). The lowest ratio the accessibility test measures on a light screen is 5.38.
4. Fixed. Dark error text is `#FF7A85`. The platform is a line of muted text under the poster, not a coloured badge. The lowest ratio the accessibility test measures on a dark screen is 4.65, in Grape.
5. Fixed. Tags on a cover are 12 px white text on a black scrim at 72% (`#B8000000`). A test holds that above 4.5 over a pure white photo.
6. Fixed. The dash is gone from the home text and from the description in `pubspec.yaml`.
7. Fixed. A video that is gone shows "Nazzil's copy of this file is gone." with Download again and Original, where the spinner used to turn for ever. On a computer, a video that nothing can open says so and says where the file is.
8. Fixed. A photo that fails to load shows "Could not load this photo." in the viewer and in the album preview. A missing photo gets the same message and buttons as a missing video.
9. Fixed. An empty library says "Nothing saved yet" with Add a link. A filter that matches nothing says "Nothing here for this filter" with Show all.
10. Fixed. A failed download shows its reason on the tile with Try again. A cancelled one shows Try again too.
11. Fixed. Every failure is one of twelve kinds, and each kind has an English and an Iraqi Arabic line (`strings.dart`, `failure`). Raw technical text no longer reaches the screen. The permission message, unused before, is the line shown when saving is refused for lack of permission.
12. Fixed. The app filter lists every app that has a download in the library, and only those.
13. Fixed. Settings, back, dismiss and play each carry a tooltip and a screen reader name.
14. Fixed. The colour swatches and the video area are built on one widget, `Tappable`, which takes keyboard focus, shows hover and pressed ink, and draws a ring while focused from the keyboard. Tabs, chips and segments use it too.
15. Fixed. Confetti, sliding highlights, page turns and screen transitions all read the system reduce motion setting and stop when it is on.
16. Fixed. Segment labels grow with the system font size, and a tile's height is worked out from the size of its text. Four tests run the screens at double text size on a 320 wide phone and find no overflow.
17. Fixed. In a window 960 wide or more, the link pane and the library sit side by side and the tabs go away. The library's column count follows the width, with tiles no wider than 220.
18. Fixed. The gradient backdrop and its blurred orbs are gone. The page is one flat neutral.

20. Fixed. The arrow on Fetch is gone.

24. Fixed. Outfit is named first, so Latin text is in Outfit in both languages, and Arabic falls through to Noto Naskh Arabic. The reasons for both are in `DESIGN.md`, Type.
25. Fixed. The three-step guide with round numbers is gone. One line of help under Fetch says what to do.
26. Fixed. Corners come in three sizes, 6, 10 and 16. The full pill is kept for the main button.
27. Fixed. One picked colour, plain neutrals, and red for errors and Delete. The accent, mint, cream and the seven platform border colours are gone.
28. Fixed. `DESIGN.md` at the repository root holds the design read, the dials and a reason for each visual choice. It is marked interim, because the remake will replace it.
29. Fixed. The count has its noun, as in "15 items". Tags show the app's name. About names all seven apps and audio. The empty library says what to do next. The seven unused strings are deleted.
30. Fixed. The five long comments are one or two lines each.
31. Fixed, and the audit's wording is corrected here. Delete removes the copy Nazzil keeps for itself. The copy in the gallery, or in Downloads/Nazzil on a computer, stays where it is. Delete now asks first, and the question says exactly that.

## Found while checking, and fixed

These were not in the audit. They turned up while the build was being read on screen and clicked through. Most have a test. At least two do not: the cover file, which was found and fixed by reading the code, and the hover shape of the play button, which was checked on screen.

The first screen review after the rework turned up smaller layout and wording faults, fixed together in commit c3d096b. The rest are listed here.

Saving one photo out of an album could write a different photo. Photo files had fixed names, and a download that met a file of the same name took it for its own unfinished work and added to it. Two posts with the same title overwrote each other the same way. Every new photo file now gets a name nothing else has.

Pause and Cancel did nothing at three moments: before the first byte arrived, while expired links were being renewed, and during a conversion, where a cancelled file was still copied to the gallery. They land at every point now.

A photo that was paused twice was counted as finished and skipped. A failed album left files behind that got in the way of the retry. A cover download could build on a leftover file. All three are fixed.

A download that was resumed showed "Waiting" where it should have shown its progress. Pause was offered during a conversion, where nothing is left to pause.

A post with one photo said "Photos", "Save photos" and "Save this photo", and the second button saved a duplicate. It has one button now, and "Photo saved" in the done dialog.

A saved photo showed a blurred site thumbnail as its cover. The poster on Home was blank while it loaded. Each tab lost its place on the page when the other was opened. The snack bar sat on top of the tabs.

On Linux, Share threw an error, so it is not offered there. Play and Open file did nothing when no program could open the file. They now say so and say where the file is. Open file had a photo-stack icon for songs and videos.

"Private or deleted" was stated as fact when a site had only refused the request. It now reads "may be private or deleted".

The Tab key jumped between the page and the tabs, and between the two panes of a wide window. It stopped on the window itself, where there is no ring to show. On a scrolled page the settings button dropped out of the round. The order is now header, page, tabs, and the round closes on the first control.

The play button in the viewer had a square hover shape on a round button.

## Left for the owner

None of these was changed. Each is a choice, or needs a device this pass did not have.

Delete keeps the copy in the gallery or in Downloads/Nazzil. If Delete should remove that copy too, say so.

Every download is stored twice on a phone: Nazzil's own copy and the gallery's.

The app locks to portrait (`lib/main.dart`). Tablets cannot rotate it.

The new Arabic lines (errors, empty states, new buttons) were written by the agent and are the owner's to correct.

On a computer the pointer stays an arrow over buttons, which is what Flutter does on Windows, macOS and Linux. The antislop rule asks for a hand, in terms written for web pages. A hand on every control is a small change if the owner wants it.

After a conversion the Linux app ignores a quit signal from the system. Whether closing its window still works was not checked. A conversion that is cancelled runs on for some seconds in the background, and the header says "Converting…" until it stops.

A cancel that arrives during the last copy into the gallery cannot undo that copy.

Pause is offered during the short tagging step of an audio download and does nothing there.

A retried album saves again the photos it had already copied to the gallery.

A photo saved alone out of an album is named after the post, not after its place in the album.

A library made before the photo-name fix may hold a photo file with mixed content. The app cannot detect or repair that. Deleting the item and downloading it again gives a clean file. The mixed copy in the gallery or in Downloads/Nazzil has to be deleted by hand.

Before download, the poster of a Reddit album on Home is the site's 140 px thumbnail.

At double text size the two tab labels wrap unevenly. Arabic body text could be a size larger. The text in the delete question breaks after "Downloads/". Post titles in unusual scripts are shown as the site gives them.

A slideshow starts at 0% where other downloads start at 2%.

If no browser exists, a credit link gives no feedback on Windows and macOS (Linux says so). Errors from Share are swallowed.

YouTube refused this server, so a YouTube download has to be confirmed on a real phone. Instagram and Facebook were not fetched live in this pass. Their parsers have tests.
