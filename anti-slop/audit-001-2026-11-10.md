# antislop audit 001

Date: 2026-11-10. Mode: AFTER. Nothing in the app was changed for this audit.

Scope: the Flutter app in `flutter/` at commit cb98ec4: every file under `lib/ui`, `lib/theme` and `lib/l10n`, plus the error and library paths in `lib/state` and `lib/data` that put text on screen.

How it was checked: code read end to end; the Linux build run at 412 x 915 in the dark and light themes (Grape colour, the saved setting); contrast computed with the WCAG formula from the `antislop-human` skill for all five colours.

Priority follows the rule tier. Hard Gate rules are HIGH, Purpose-Gate rules are MEDIUM, Quality Locks are LOW. Fixes happen only for the numbers the owner approves.

## HIGH

1. R-25. White text on the main button, the active tab and the active choice is too faint in all five colours: rose 3.14, ocean 3.89, emerald 2.55, sunset 2.74, grape 4.23. Normal text needs 4.5. `nazzil_button.dart`, `app.dart`, `segmented.dart`, `quality_switch.dart`.
2. R-25. White text on the accent button (Save photos, Share, the active Photos choice) is worse: rose 2.14, ocean 1.52, emerald 1.39, sunset 1.62, grape 1.88. `home_screen.dart`, `done_sheet.dart`, `quality_switch.dart`.
3. R-25, R-34. The light theme fails in several places. The Ready status is 1.5, and 1.03 to 1.18 where it sits inside the corner glow. Coloured text on white (Paste, the update notice) is 2.55 to 4.23. Badges are 1.3 to 3.5. Error text is 3.7. Muted text is 4.37. `nazzil_theme.dart`, `app.dart`, `paste_card.dart`, `home_screen.dart`.
4. R-25. Dark theme: error text on the result card is 4.46, and the platform badge is 3.74 in Grape and 4.06 in Ocean. `home_screen.dart`.
5. R-25. Library tile chips set 10 px coloured text on a see-through black chip. Over a bright photo the ratio falls to between 1.1 and 3.3. `library_tile.dart`.
6. R-02. Em dash in step 2 of the home guide, in English and Arabic (`strings.dart:101`), and in the app description (`pubspec.yaml:2`).
7. R-27. The video viewer has no error state. If the file is gone or cannot be opened, the spinner never ends. `viewer_screen.dart`.
8. R-27. A missing photo in the viewer, or a photo that fails to load in the album preview, shows only an icon: no text and no action. `viewer_screen.dart`, `photo_film.dart`.
9. R-27. The library shows the same "Nothing saved yet" for an empty library and for a filter that matches nothing. Neither says what to do next. `library_screen.dart`.
10. R-27. A failed download says "Download failed" and offers only Original and Delete. There is no retry. `library_tile.dart`, `library_screen.dart`.
11. R-27. Error messages are English only, so the Arabic interface shows English errors, and an unrecognised error prints its raw technical text. A translated permission message exists in `strings.dart` and is never used. `extractor.dart`, `downloader.dart`.
12. R-26. The library's app filter lists YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook. Downloads from Reddit, X and Pinterest cannot be filtered. `library_screen.dart`.
13. R-39. Three icon-only buttons have no name for screen readers: settings and back, dismiss the clipboard offer, and the play button for videos that open in another player. `app.dart`, `home_screen.dart`, `viewer_screen.dart`.
14. R-32, R-39. The colour swatches and the tap-to-pause video area are bare gesture detectors: no keyboard focus, no hover state, no pressed state. This matters on Windows, macOS and Linux. `settings_screen.dart`, `viewer_screen.dart`.
15. R-39. No animation checks the system reduce-motion setting: the confetti, the sliding highlights, the album preview scaling.
16. R-03. Text does not follow the system font size cleanly. Segmented labels shrink to fit instead of growing, and library titles run into the next row at large sizes because every tile has a fixed 0.62 shape. `segmented.dart`, `library_screen.dart`.
17. R-03. Computers get the phone layout, capped at 640 px, at every window size. The library stays two columns however wide the window is. `app.dart`.

## MEDIUM

18. R-01. The page background is a tinted gradient with two or three blurred colour orbs. In Grape that is purple on black, and the light themes are pastel washes. No purpose is written down. `glow_backdrop.dart`.
19. R-13. Glow sits on four things at once: the backdrop orbs, the logo tile, the step numbers and the active photo card. The cap is one or two.
20. R-08. The arrow on Fetch is decoration. `home_screen.dart:47`.
21. R-04. The SD choice shows an HD icon, because every video option uses the same one. `quality_switch.dart`.
22. R-09. The platform badge appears twice on one result, on the poster and again under it. The seven platform pills under Fetch repeat the paste hint and look tappable but do nothing. `home_screen.dart`, `platform_pills.dart`.
23. R-19. Confetti fires on every finished download, with no purpose written down. `done_sheet.dart`.
24. R-06. English body text renders in the Latin letters of the Arabic font, a serif, because the two body styles name NotoNaskhArabic first. English screens therefore mix Outfit with a serif nobody chose. No reason is written for Outfit either. `nazzil_theme.dart`.

## LOW

25. R-05. "Three beats" is the numbered three-step guide with round gradient numbers. `home_screen.dart`.
26. R-11. Every shape is a pill or a large-radius box: buttons and chips 999, cards 22 to 32, the input 26, the tab bar 28. There is no radius scale.
27. R-29. Colours in play at once: primary, accent, mint, cream, error red, and seven platform border colours. The accent works as a second brand colour, not as an accent.
28. R-20, R-31, R-37. There is no `DESIGN.md` and no written reason for any visual choice. With the logo swapped, the screens could belong to any app.
29. R-16. Vague or stale text. A bare "10" sits under Library with no noun. Tile chips show raw ids such as "pinterest" and "reddit". About mentions video, TikTok photos and Instagram carousels, and leaves out audio, Reddit, X and Pinterest. The empty-library hint "Photos stay photos" gives no next step. Seven strings are never used: `platforms`, `updateCheck`, `updateCurrent`, `updateLater`, `photoCount`, `openSettings`, `permissionNeeded`.
30. antislop-code. Five doc comments run three or four lines, and the skill allows two: `ffmpeg_args.dart:100`, `id3.dart:5`, `gallery.dart:39`, `media_convert.dart:20`, `downloader.dart:262`. No banners, step narration or emoji turned up.

## Outside the rule list

31. Delete in the library removes the saved file at once, with no confirmation and no undo. `library_screen.dart`, `nazzil_controller.dart`.

## Checked and clean

No invented statistics, testimonials or claims (R-17, R-18, R-36, R-38). The credits link to real pages. The theme switch offers system, light and dark (R-21); the light theme's failures are finding 3. The update prompt only appears on Android, where the link it opens is valid (R-26).
