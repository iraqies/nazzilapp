# antislop remake 003

Date: 2026-11-11. Mode: AFTER. Follows `remake-002-2026-11-11.md`.

Scope: the Flutter app in `flutter/`, commits 9b1502f to eaeed4b on the branch `cursor/nazzil-fix-a100`. `main` is untouched.

What was asked, in the owner's order. He picked logo 13 out of twenty-five concepts, the word نزّل in the Ruqaa hand, and asked for the whole app in that lettering. He sent the Anthropic Sans files for English, then asked for an English typeface that matches the Arabic one. He found that the pink accent stuck out, and picked Grape from sixteen colours shown on the app's own screens. Last, he asked that the app allow no other colour. Four builds went to him on the way: 2.1 (24), 2.2 (25), 2.3 (26) and 2.4 (27).

How it was checked: 157 automated tests pass on eaeed4b (`flutter test`) and the analyzer reports no issues. Linux release builds of 2.1 were clicked through. Each of the four versions was run on an Android 14 emulator: for 2.2, 2.3 and 2.4 the APK that went to the owner, for 2.1 a build two small fixes before it. The APK of 2.4 was built from eaeed4b and inspected: package `com.nazzil.app`, version 2.4 (27), arm64, signed with this machine's debug key like the three before it, with the last two fixes in it. No build was run on a phone, because there is none here.

Who wrote this: the agent that took the work over after the last build was made, and that sent that build to the owner. The runs were made by the agents before it, all but the one under "After delivery". What this report says about theirs rests on the commit messages, on the logs and screen captures the runs left on the build machine, and on what the owner was told with each build. The captures of the last build were read one by one for this report. Where a line rests on less than that, it says so. Those logs and captures are no longer on the machine: its scratch directory was cleared on 2026-11-12, after this report was written. What the owner was sent is in the chat.

## What changed

The logo is the word نزّل in the Ruqaa hand. It stands in the header where the name was set as text, with its two dots and its shadda in Grape. `design/logo/export.py` writes it from the outlines of the typeface: the outline the header paints, the launcher icons of Android, iOS, macOS and Windows, the launch screen, and the small icon of the notification.

Arabic is set in Aref Ruqaa, the hand of the logo. English is set in Fraunces, picked out of nineteen typefaces set beside the Arabic. `design/type/build.py` makes the app's copies of both, and `DESIGN.md`, Type, gives the reasons. The Anthropic Sans files were in the app for two commits (6120fb3 and a033a44) and have been out of it since.

The launch screen shows the word on the page colour of the theme the app is set to, and stays until the saved language and theme are read.

The accent is Grape, and the app has no other. Rose went out in 2.2. The choice of colour went out of Settings in 2.4, with its words and its stored choice. A phone that had another colour picked opens in Grape, because the stored choice is no longer read, and two tests hold that. The contrast tests ran once for each colour and run once now, which is why 2.4 has fewer tests than the 168 of 2.3.

On Android the app keeps the phone's Back on every page. How a download ended (Saved, Paused, Download failed) is a notification of its own, and the notification of a running download goes when its service goes.

- The launch screen followed the phone's day or night, so the dark app came up behind a light page. The app tells the phone its theme at each start and each change, on Android 12 and later (f2c4199). Seen on the emulator in Dark, Light and System.
- The first frame was drawn before the saved settings were read, so an app set to Arabic or Light opened on a moment of English and dark. The launch screen stays until they are in (ee84a02, with a test).
- A GIF post's second choice read "GIF file" in the Arabic app. It reads ملف GIF (f2527b0, with a test).
- A download opened on 2%, fell to 0% and climbed from there. It holds the opening mark until the count passes it (d4e22fe, with a test).
- Fraunces is about an eighth wider than the typeface before it, and the last of the five colours in Settings fell onto a second row. The row was tightened (11d0ece). The row is gone since 2.4.

In 2.2

- After Resume the poster showed 2% until the first bytes came: 39%, then 2%, then 40%. A resumed download opens on the percentage it was paused at (5d4447d, with tests). Seen on a Linux build through a slow connection: 31% at the pause, 32% after Resume.

In 2.3

- In Settings the phone's Back left the app, and had done so since 2.0. It goes back to the page (c071853, with a test).

In 2.4

- An app swiped away from the recent apps while it downloaded left its notification on a percentage, with a Pause that reached no one and a lock that kept the phone awake. The service is stopped when the page is closed, and its notification goes with it (eaeed4b). Seen on the emulator.

## Left for the owner

Each of these is a choice, or needs something this pass did not have.

Purple on a black page is a look many generated interfaces share. Grape is his pick out of sixteen colours, and he was told this when 2.2 went to him. `DESIGN.md`, Colour, holds how it was picked and where it is allowed to go.

The English typeface is Fraunces. The sheet he was sent shows five others set the same way, and `DESIGN.md` says what a switch touches.

The Anthropic Sans files carry no licence. They are out of the app and still in the history of this repository (6120fb3), which matters only if the repository is made public.

The very first time the app is opened, the launch screen follows the phone's day or night, because the app has not told the phone anything yet. Before Android 12 the phone cannot be told at all, and the launch screen is always the dark one.

A download lives with the app's page. Back and Home leave it running. Swiping the app away from the recent apps ends it, and it starts again when the app is next opened. Carrying a download on without the page means moving it into the Android service, which is a larger change than this pass made. A download resumed after the app was closed opens on 2% until its first bytes.

An album of photos turned into a video was seen to its end only after 2.4 had gone to him. He was told it was open when the build went, and how it ended once that was known. The run record has it under "After delivery". The code that converts was not touched in this pass. It asks the phone's H.264 encoder first, at 1080 wide and then at 720, and falls back to MPEG-4. The emulator's encoder took the first of the three. A phone has its maker's encoder in that place, so the first album made on a phone is worth a look.

Nothing in this pass ran on a real phone. The launch screen, the notifications, Back, sharing a post into Nazzil and the gallery were seen on the emulator only. YouTube refuses this server, so a YouTube download still has to be confirmed on a phone.

On a computer, 2.2 to 2.4 were not run. The last Linux build clicked through was 2.1 with the Resume fix. What changed after it is held by the tests, which run the screens as a wide window too. The Windows, macOS and iOS builds are made on GitHub, where this machine cannot see them.

On the Linux build of 2.1 a request to close the window was sometimes not followed while the machine was under heavy load, and a second request closed it: 2 of 16 through the window manager, 5 of 16 when sent straight to the window. The app has no code on that path, and the cause was not found. On the same build Escape does not close the viewer, and Back does.

The notes of the 2.1 click-through say that three English lines take a second line on a phone 360 wide. They do not say which lines. The tests find no overflow at 320 wide.

Still standing from remake 002: the Arabic choices, the site that lists 1.3, the debug signing key, the lock to portrait, Delete leaving the gallery copy, and what was carried over from fix 001. The launcher icon and the name set as text, listed there, are what this pass replaced.
