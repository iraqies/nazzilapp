## What changed

The Home showcase copied Finery's row of badges as it stands, and Finery gilds the first badge of that row to put its main product forward. YouTube came first in Nazzil's row, so it took the gold. The apps a link can come from are equals, so the gold went: every badge is the same plain pill, and the three gold colours left the theme. `DESIGN.md`, Colour, says so.

A new test in `flutter/test/screens_test.dart` fails if one badge is set apart from the others again. It was run against 6664259, the commit before the fix, and failed there with two looks among the badges.
