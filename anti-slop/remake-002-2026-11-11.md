# antislop remake 002

Date: 2026-11-11. Mode: AFTER. Follows `fix-001-2026-11-10.md`.

Scope: the Flutter app in `flutter/`, commits a8401a1 to c4e2173 on the branch `cursor/nazzil-fix-a100`. `main` is untouched.

What was asked: the owner picked Cinema out of four drawn directions (`design/remake/`), with every function of the app kept. He then asked for the Arabic to be standard Arabic and not Iraqi dialect. The 25 logo concepts he asked for at the same time are a separate delivery and are not part of this report.

How it was checked: 159 automated tests pass (`flutter test`) and the analyzer reports no issues. The Linux release build was run and clicked through at 412 x 915 in both themes and both languages, and at 1320 x 920. The Android APK was built from c4e2173 and inspected: package `com.nazzil.app`, version 2.0 (23), arm64, Android 8 and later, signed with this machine's debug key like the test APK before it. It was not run on a phone, because there is none here.

## What changed

The design is the one in `DESIGN.md`: a black and white interface where the picture is the only colour, a picture that goes grey when its download starts and takes its colour back as the file arrives, an empty frame that asks for a link, and a library that is a wall of frames. The functions are the ones the app had: seven apps, video, photos, albums and audio, pause, resume, cancel, retry, the library with its filters, the viewer, sharing into Nazzil, the update check, two languages, three theme settings and five colours.

The version is 2.0 (23). The published app is 1.3, and the number in Settings is how the two are told apart.

The Arabic is standard Arabic in every line. A count takes the form its number asks for. The library is called التنزيلات, after the verb the app is named for. On Android the download notification is now in the app's language, where it was English whatever the app was set to.

## Found while clicking, and fixed

Each of these turned up in the real build. Each has a test that fails on the old code, except the first, which was confirmed on the build itself, and the last, which needs a phone.

After a conversion the Linux app ignored a quit signal, so a computer shutting down could not close it. FFmpeg took the signals for itself. It is told to leave them, and the app closes (832bdf0). Fix 001 had left this open.

A download that was paused and resumed dropped to the bottom of the library and out of the strip of last saves. It keeps its place now, and the one resumed last still runs next (278b9d9).

With no connection, an X link said the post may be private or deleted, and Pinterest and Reddit said they could not load it. All three now say the connection dropped (725cde2).

An album fetched a second time opened on the photo it had last been left on, while the count said 1 of 3 and Save this photo saved the first one. The cause was one stored scroll position shared by the page and the things inside it. The photo pager follows the count alone now, and the link field and the strip of last saves keep positions of their own (1b11d5a).

In Arabic a tile read 8% and the sheet of the same download read %8. The percentage keeps its sign on one side now (fbf86cd).

In Arabic the button said نزّل and the dialog said تم التنزيل, while the tab and the strip said التحميلات, a word the app also used for a picture loading. The library is التنزيلات (649459c).

A saved video's sheet did not say which quality it was saved in, so two saves of one video looked the same. It says so now (c28e731).

The Android notification, its three buttons and its channel name were English in the Arabic app. The app now hands Android its words at start and on a language switch (c4e2173). This one was found by reading the code. A test holds the two word lists together. Nobody has seen the notification on a phone yet.

## Left for the owner

Each of these is a choice, or needs a device this pass did not have.

The Arabic was rewritten by the agent and is the owner's to correct. Three choices in it are his to confirm: buttons stay commands (نزّل, الصق, احذف) and are not nouns (تنزيل, لصق, حذف), the name is written نزل without its shadda as before, and the library is التنزيلات where it was التحميلات.

The launcher icon is the old one, and the app shows its name as text. A logo is the next delivery.

The site still lists 1.3 as the latest version, so this build offers no update. Publishing 2.0 there is the owner's step.

The APK is signed with a debug key. It installs over the test APK sent before. Over a copy from the site it may conflict, and Android then asks for the old one to be removed first.

The app locks to portrait (`flutter/lib/main.dart`). Tablets cannot rotate it.

Delete removes the copy Nazzil keeps and leaves the gallery copy. If Delete should remove both, say so.

On a computer, in a narrow window, the strip of last saves moves with a trackpad or the keyboard and not by dragging with a mouse. The Library link beside it shows everything.

On a computer the pointer stays an arrow over buttons, as Flutter does on Windows, macOS and Linux.

YouTube refused this server, so a YouTube download has to be confirmed on a real phone. Instagram and Facebook were not fetched live in this pass. Their parsers have tests.

Carried over from fix 001 and not looked at again in this pass: every download is stored twice on a phone, a cancel during the last copy into the gallery cannot undo that copy, Pause does nothing during the short tagging step of an audio download, a retried album saves again the photos it had already copied, a photo saved alone out of an album is named after the post, and a library made before the photo-name fix may hold a mixed file that only a fresh download repairs.
